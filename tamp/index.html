TAMP — Ethernet Cable

Our new Cat8 Ethernet cable is built for high-end audio streaming where signal integrity is essential.

It employs a premium CAT 8.1 construction with advanced shielding: aluminum PETP foil around each twisted copper pair, reinforced by a specialized thermoplastic layer and an outer aerospace-grade noise-suppressing sleeve. This design delivers outstanding interference rejection while maintaining excellent high-frequency performance.

Fitted with robust die-cast zinc RJ45 connectors and secure latch protection, the cable ensures stable, low-loss connections in every situation.

This cable is the perfect match for our flagship Ethernet isolator STILL, creating a complete high-end network solution when used together. Simple yet sonically refined, it serves as an excellent complement to our more advanced products.

Specifications

  • CAT 8.1 Cable & Connectors
  • RJ45 connectors
  • Available in 1m and 2m lengths
